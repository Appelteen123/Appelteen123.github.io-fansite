About American horror story

American Horror Story is an anthology series playing in different locations and time periods. For example, a murder house, an asylum, a coven, a freak show, a hotel, a Roanoke farmhouse, a cult, the apocalypse, a summer camp, Provincetown, and a mothership. It features different stories and characters in each season.

Description

The Harmons face many conflicts going on in their new home; the primary one stemming from Ben Harmon's (Dylan McDermott) adultery, and the subsequent fallout with his wife Vivien (Connie Britton) and daughter Violet (Taissa Farmiga). Other conflicts include the intrusive behavior of the neighbors, Constance (Jessica Lange) and Adelaide (Jamie Brewer), as well as intrusions from one of Dr. Harmon's patients, Tate (Evan Peters). A parade of mysterious visitors to their haunted home, including the scarred Larry Harvey (Denis O'Hare), make the first year of the Harmons in Los Angeles very dangerous. The house seems to have a vivid history, ranging from brutal murders to demonic manifestations, and it seems to have its own agenda.

Description

Teenager Zoe Benson discovers that she is a witch and is sent to Miss Robichaux's Academy in New Orleans. There she joins a coven of young witches led by Cordelia Foxx, while Cordelia's mother Fiona, the current Supreme, returns to protect her power. The witches become caught in a conflict with the voodoo priestess Marie Laveau and the cruel immortal Delphine LaLaurie, while an ancient rivalry threatens the future of the coven.

Season 4:

Description

In 1952, Elsa Mars runs one of the last surviving freak shows in Jupiter, Florida. She recruits conjoined twins Bette and Dot Tattler, hoping their arrival will save her struggling troupe. As the performers fight to survive and find acceptance, they are threatened by the murderous clown Twisty and the wealthy, disturbed Dandy Mott. Rivalry, exploitation and dark secrets slowly tear the carnival family apart.

Description

The Hotel Cortez in Los Angeles is home to ghosts, vampires and a long history of murder. Detective John Lowe investigates a series of brutal killings while the mysterious Countess rules the hotel with her dangerous companions. As John searches for the truth, the hotel residents struggle with addiction, jealousy and revenge. The building seems to trap everyone who enters, keeping its darkest secrets alive.

Season 6:

Wikipedia
IMDb

Description

Matt and Shelby Miller move to a remote farmhouse in North Carolina after a traumatic incident in Los Angeles. Their experiences are retold in the paranormal documentary My Roanoke Nightmare, which explores the violent spirits haunting the property. When the original residents and actors return for a follow-up show, the boundary between television and reality disappears. The house becomes the center of a bloody struggle involving the Butcher, the Polk family and an ancient witch.

Description

After the 2016 presidential election, Ally Mayfair-Richards' fears and phobias become increasingly intense when a group of masked clowns begins terrorizing her neighborhood. At the same time, Kai Anderson uses public fear and political division to build a dangerous cult. As violence spreads through Brookfield Heights, Ally fights to protect her family and uncover who is controlling the chaos. Unlike most seasons, this story contains no supernatural elements.

Description

After nuclear missiles destroy much of the world, a group of survivors lives under strict rules in an underground outpost. Their fragile safety is threatened when Michael Langdon arrives and reveals his plan for the future. The witches of New Orleans must return to face him, the Antichrist born from Murder House. Description

In 1984, a group of young counselors arrives at Camp Redwood, a summer camp reopening after a horrific massacre. Brooke, Montana, Xavier, Chet and Ray soon discover that the camp is haunted by its violent past and a relentless killer known as Mr. Jingles. Their fight for survival becomes connected to the Night Stalker and the camp's vengeful ghosts. Old crimes, betrayal and unfinished grief keep Camp Redwood trapped in a deadly cycle.

Season 10:

Wikipedia
IMDb

Description

Double Feature tells two separate horror stories. In Red Tide, writer Harry Gardner travels with his family to Provincetown, where mysterious black pills can unlock great talent but create a terrible hunger. In Death Valley, a group of students becomes part of a decades-long alien conspiracy involving secret government deals and human experimentation. Together, the two stories explore ambition, fear and the price of becoming extraordinary.

Description

In 1980s New York City, journalist Gino Barelli investigates a series of murders targeting gay men while detective Patrick Read struggles with secrets in his personal and professional life. At the same time, a mysterious new virus begins spreading through the city's queer community. The killer, the disease and the terrifying figure known as Big Daddy create a growing atmosphere of fear. The season follows a community fighting for truth, safety and survival.

Description

Actress Anna Victoria Alcott finally becomes pregnant after a difficult IVF journey, but her happiness is quickly overshadowed by disturbing visions and a group of mysterious women following her.
